High-Quality Streaming Ortho

Satellite imagery streams in as you fly from popular mapping services to create the world around you.

Scenery packages carry only the terrain data, ensuring whole regions easily fit on your disk.

{{ partial "icon.html" (dict "name" "night" "size" 48) }}

Cities Light Up at Night

Ortho scenery used to leave cities in the dark after sunset. XEarthLayer lights urban life using real-world luminance data from NASA's Black Marble program combined with the existing X-Plane night lighting artwork.

NASA's luminance data ensures that large city centers glow brighter than residential suburbs, and the lighting color adopts the appropriate hue for the type of district across the globe.

{{ partial "icon.html" (dict "name" "packages" "size" 48) }}

Easy Package Management

Choose the regions you want to fly in a full-screen TUI package manager, ensuring that installing, updating or removing global scenery is easy. Fly with SimHeaven X-World Pro? XEarthLayer steps aside for it on the continents it covers.

{{ partial "icon.html" (dict "name" "dashboard" "size" 48) }}

Live Terminal Dashboard

See what is happening as you fly: cache use, downloads, your aircraft's position, pipeline health and X-Plane's frame rate, all in your terminal.

{{ partial "icon.html" (dict "name" "prefetch" "size" 48) }}

Efficient Prefetching

XEarthLayer follows your aircraft through X-Plane and prepares the world before the simulator needs it, reducing sim stalls, stutters and freezes that can occur with these systems.

Already know your flight route before departure? XEarthLayer can preload the departure scenery before you start flying for faster startup times.

{{ partial "icon.html" (dict "name" "encoding" "size" 48) }}

Hardware Acceleration

Textures are compressed for X-Plane in real time using hardware acceleration on the CPU, five to ten times faster than plain software encoding. If you have a spare integrated GPU, XEarthLayer uses this to encode even faster while leaving your main graphics card for X-Plane.

Before it downloads a region, XEarthLayer checks that the disk holding your packages has room for about three times the download, which covers extraction with margin, and that the temporary folder has room for the download itself. When both are on the same disk the two add up, which is the figure above. Night lighting reserves a further 5% of the cache capacity above. Every region and its size is listed on the Package Management page.
