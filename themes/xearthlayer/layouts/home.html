High-Quality Streaming Ortho

Satellite imagery streams in as you fly and is built into full-resolution textures on your machine. Packages carry only the terrain, so a whole region fits in a few gigabytes, and five of the imagery providers need no account.

{{ partial "icon.html" (dict "name" "night" "size" 48) }}

Cities Light Up at Night

Ortho scenery used to leave cities dark after sunset. XEarthLayer lights them from NASA's night lights and your own X-Plane art, so a large city glows brighter than a small one and open country stays dark.

{{ partial "icon.html" (dict "name" "packages" "size" 48) }}

Easy Package Management

Choose from seventeen regions in a full-screen package manager, and install, update or remove them in a few keystrokes. Fly with SimHeaven X-World Pro? XEarthLayer steps aside for it on the continents it covers.

{{ partial "icon.html" (dict "name" "dashboard" "size" 48) }}

Live Terminal Dashboard

See what is happening as you fly: cache use, downloads, your aircraft's position, pipeline health and X-Plane's frame rate, all in your terminal.

{{ partial "icon.html" (dict "name" "prefetch" "size" 48) }}

Efficient Prefetching

XEarthLayer follows your aircraft through X-Plane and loads tiles ahead of it, in a lane of its own that never delays a tile X-Plane is waiting for. It holds back on takeoff, so departure scenery loads first.

{{ partial "icon.html" (dict "name" "encoding" "size" 48) }}

SIMD and GPU Acceleration

Textures are compressed with SIMD kernels, five to ten times faster than plain software encoding. With a spare GPU, XEarthLayer can encode there instead and leave your main card to X-Plane.

Before it downloads a region, XEarthLayer checks that the disk holding your packages has room for about three times the download, which covers extraction with margin, and that the temporary folder has room for the download itself. When both are on the same disk the two add up, which is the figure above. Night lighting reserves a further 5% of the cache capacity above. Every region and its size is listed on the Package Management page.
